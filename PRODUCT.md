# Product context

## Register
Product: an authenticated personal life RPG, with dashboard, quests, reports,
history and settings. Challenges extends that workflow to two participants.

## Users and purpose
The existing app helps signed-in users record habits, quests and personal progress
on desktop and mobile. Challenges lets two participants agree on private
commitments and track their own progress together. Preserve existing personal
quest behavior while making the new screen feel part of the same application.

## Existing direction
Source of truth: UI-V2.md, src/index.css, src/styles.module.css and the existing
Quests and Reports screens. The app uses a celestial visual language, restrained
blue actions, quiet dark surfaces and readable hierarchy. UI-V2.md explicitly
removes franchise vocabulary and keeps decorative glow subdued. Consistency with
these shipped screens takes precedence over introducing a new visual identity.

## Interaction principles
Use accessible labels, keyboard focus, responsive layouts and clear error/loading
states as required by the Challenges brief. Keep readable body copy, descriptive
control names and account boundaries intact. Do not replace the existing app's
navigation or personal workflows during a visual consistency pass.
